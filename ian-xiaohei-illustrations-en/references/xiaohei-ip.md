# Xiaohei (the default IP character)

> This is the **default** profile. To use your own character instead, see "Bring your own character" in `SKILL.md` and copy `references/custom-ip-template.md` to `references/custom-ip.md`.

## Definition

Xiaohei is the fixed visual IP of these illustrations. By default, every image includes Xiaohei. Xiaohei is not a mascot, a sticker, or cute decoration — it is an absurd worker earnestly keeping the system running.

## Form

- A small solid-black creature.
- White dot eyes.
- Thin legs, occasionally thin arms.
- The body can be a cylinder, a black bean, a black box, a funnel, a shadow, a hole, or a black block inside a machine.
- Slightly irregular, hand-drawn outline.
- Expression: blank, dull, calm, earnest.

## Personality

- Very earnest, but doing something slightly absurd.
- Like a low-key system operator.
- Dry humor, never cutesy.
- A bit clumsy, but not stupid.
- Looks like it genuinely owns some job inside a whiteboard sketch.

## Common jobs (let Xiaohei carry the core action)

- Carrying material.
- Pulling lines to converge sources.
- Stuck at a break point.
- Working a "judgment" lever inside a machine.
- Becoming a sorting funnel.
- Cutting open a "material fish".
- Stamping a handoff line.
- Leading a handoff path.
- Holding a warning sign over a pit.
- Reaching out of a hole but failing to catch the content.
- Hauling bricks, building bridges, opening doors, sorting, recording.

## Forbidden

- Don't draw Xiaohei as an overly cute mascot.
- Don't make it a children's cartoon character.
- Don't give it complex clothing, emoji faces, or shiny eyes.
- Don't let it just stand in a corner watching.
- Don't let it steal the structural expression.
- Don't make it too commercial, too round, or too polished.

## The test (load-bearing)

If you remove Xiaohei and the core metaphor still fully holds, then Xiaohei was too decorative — rewrite the prompt so Xiaohei becomes the subject of the action. This is the single most important rule of the whole skill: the character must *do* the idea, not illustrate next to it.
